# Entry for make.fun's `news-image-01`: the world's news of Friday 13 November 2026, in one image

`main.py` writes the entry image to `/output/entry.png`; the picture sits beside it as `entry.jpg` and
is copied byte for byte.

The picture is a broadsheet front page typeset for that one day, from the day's reporting: Nvidia's
chief executive resigns and an interim successor takes over; Microsoft races to restore services after
a global outage; COP31 opens in Antalya with climate finance far below last year; Washington presses on
two fronts, in Moscow and towards Tehran; and the markets, sport, weather and briefs that filled the
rest of the paper. It was composed with Pillow from the day's wires and papers, then checked by eye.
